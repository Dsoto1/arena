Designer's Note

Within the Magnus edition, the world of Flavius Rule has now expanded to over 60 pages!
Though fear not the weight of this rulebook, as it simply mirrors the vastness of your potential - as is such, let us unshackle them together, one revelation at a time!

Well over half of the Magnus edition is dedicated to setup and expansions, with core rules consisting of ~25 pages. Although it would be very helpful for at least one participant to know most(if not all) of the rules, many playthroughs can be easily achieved with only a rudimentary understanding of setup and core mechanics(movement, attacking, turn order, etc.).

I trust you have enough patience and understanding to know that the pages included are only to benefit the personalized experience you devise within this sandbox of gladiatorial survival. Each page or section will highlight related rules or situations that a player could be presented with and should be used for reference while in play. Given the aforementioned, it is suggested to use following approach for a majority of Flavius' events:

Pre-Game:
[Pages 2-12] Overview, Components, Setup, Turn Phases, Winning
[Pages 36-53] Choose Class, Location(Event/Summa Rudis)

During Game:
[Pages 11-35, 67] Core Mechanics, Quick Reference

Post-Game:
[Pages 54+] Fate, Missios, Infamis, Character Cards, Notes
