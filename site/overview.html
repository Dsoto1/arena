Overview

Gameplay Overview

In an extraordinary twist of fate, individuals plucked from the monotony of their everyday lives find themselves thrust into a tapestry of gripping arena events, all under the auspices of the renowned sponsor Flavius. These valiant souls are charged with the harrowing task of surviving amidst fellow gladiators, each caught in a similar web of circumstances. Such a spectacle, bathed in the cruel delight of Flavius and embraced by the adoration of the boisterous crowds, compels a profound question to arise: Shall the simmering tensions drive these warriors to defy the sponsor's whims, seeking individual glory? Or shall they dutifully surrender and satisfy the insatiable desires of Flavius' rule?

Flavius Rule is a turn-based gladiatorial strategy game for 4x players (with other events supporting 1-8x players)

Armed with but a humble class loadout, each player embarks upon a relentless gladiatorial odyssey to mold their character into an indomitable force. Seek out shiny new armor and weapons, glistening coins, strategic tokens, and the ever-fickle favor of the enraptured crowd.

But beware! For within this perilous arena, the path to triumph is fraught with peril and risk. Navigating these treacherous arenas, players shall encounter the watchful eye of the Summa Rudis, the enigmatic master of ceremonies. They should brace for thrilling encounters as they clash with savage arena beasts and formidable adversaries. Always on guard against the pesky spectators whose fickle whims can sway the tides of fortune. Within these walls of ambition, the ultimate test awaits – each must vanquish their rivals and emerge as the sole survivor, the last gladiator standing tall amidst the fallen. In this riveting contest of cunning and courage, only the boldest and most resilient warriors shall claim victory. Who will rise to the occasion, defying the odds and proving their mettle? Or who will succumb to the relentless onslaught and be condemned to obscurity? The stage is set, dear players. The time has come to forge a legacy encompassed by the thunderous roar of a Flavius Rule crowd.

To win Flavius Rule(classic): Be the last remaining player
