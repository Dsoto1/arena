AUT VIAM INVENIAM AUT FACIAM

Behold! I implore you all to embark on any daring quests of creativity one can devise, embracing even
the most obscure ideas, for the wisdom gained on these paths shall surpass any mere creation.
In the year 2016, I set forth on a momentous journey that has forged the very essence of Flavius Rule
as you now behold it. From humble beginnings, tiny sparks of inspiration ignited, propelling me into
uncharted territories where new, exhilarating challenges awaited. As I now reach the culmination of
this odyssey, should this game kindle even a flicker of entertainment, joy, or inspiration within a select
few, I shall consider my mission triumphantly fulfilled, surpassing all initial aspirations.

I extend profound gratitude to my esteemed Flavius community, the valiant fr-qa-team,
the venerable Indie Game Lab, and the illustrious board game design communities for their invaluable
contributions, their sagacious counsel, and the endless wellspring of provided inspiration.
Furthermore, I humbly express boundless love and reverence to my father,
who lent his guiding hand in designing and constructing the very first prototype of Flavius Rule,
forever etching his legacy into this immersive realm.

Finally, I pour my heartfelt appreciation to each and every one of you, noble
players, for it is through your participation that the spirit of Flavius Rule is brought
to life. May your ventures within this ancient realm resonate with valor, cunning,
and a glimmer of the divine. God bless & Bonam fortunam!
