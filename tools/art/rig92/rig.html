How to pose

Drag a joint to turn it (spine, shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, head). Drag a wrist to reach (arm IK). Drag an ankle to move that foot on the base (pinned) or reach (free). Drag the pink pelvis to shift the hips; pinned feet stay put. Pink hand tips turn the wrist. Right-drag / wheel = orbit / zoom.
