Name of the merged block: Forum (the marketplace where Romans bought, sold and struck deals — Market, Crowd Favor, Loot, Trade · Truce all live there). Alternates: Macellum (the Roman market hall — strong on buying, weaker on deals) · Negotium (Latin "business, dealings" — strong on trade/truce, weaker on the market). Its strip blends the old Deal parchment into the Market leather.
Weapons: one big button per equipped weapon (icon, name, short damage · range); the docked ◎ on its right = Focus with that weapon (3+ movement, uses all that is left; dimmed under 3). A throwable weapon would show a small ↗ in the same dock below Focus — not drawn here. Key A still cycles the weapons.
