🛡 The body p.22

Head · chest · two arms · two legs, each with its own HP (max 7) and the AP of the armor worn there.

An arm or leg at 0 HP is severed — its armor and weapon fall. Head or chest at 0: out.

Digital Focus on a pillar's Blessings: +1 damage on a hit.

💼 Manager Guide

Diversify: one great fighter dies by volume. Keep three starters, one per bout, and a healthy bench.

🏋 The Training Yard

Lock in sessions every week; the Doctore's drills add 10% and more with each yard level. Watch fatigue above 60.

🩹 Infirmary & Armory

Rest weeks cost a bout but save a career. Repairs are a fifth of the price — mend before Match Week.

💰 Money

Gate share grows with fame. Wins pay up to ×3 with upsets and streaks; loans come only from the syndicate.

🏆 League prizes

Season stakes from 120 coin for the top house; byes in weeks 4 and 11 are your free training.
