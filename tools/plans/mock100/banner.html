🚩 One Banner Press

Nine-odd banner systems today, drawn nine ways. In v1.0 they all come off one press: one heraldry (cloth, device, motto, sculpt, trim, wear) and five kinds — Flag · Banner · Pennant · Tapestry · Crest. The kind is only the shape and the frame; the house picks its heraldry once and every place in the game derives its own kind. The bar is the online Domus-rank pennant: woven cloth, folds, gold edge, tassels.

§1

Today — every banner in the game

rendered from the built game (v0.99)

Same engine underneath most of them (flagURI: a 600×340 rectangle, a watermark device at 45% opacity, letters at the bottom) — but each caller adds its own frame, or none. The tapestries and wall banners are canvas code with their own colours; the room banner is a hand-drawn SVG; the rank pennants are the only ones with a real frame.

§3

One design, many uses

a house picks ONE heraldry; each place derives its kind

Who owns which heraldry: Manager house = m.flag (rivals derive theirs as today, from id + city) · online Domus = D.look89 grown to a full heraldry (server keeps the small JSON; the rank pennant stays the system design, the house crest rides on its top bar from Ludus Magnus up) · Studio = customFlags, chosen by a character (banner91), a lanista or a career. Families keep their derived heraldry (kin share it).

Small sizes — one rule for every kind

Under 44 px: no texture, no motto, no sculpt detail, a thicker edge — chips stay legible in the league table and room lists. Images are cached per design + kind + size, so a table of 12 houses is 12 draws once.
