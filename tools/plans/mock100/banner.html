🦅 Heraldry — one press for every signum

Round 2, made Roman. One house design — cloth, pattern, emblem, inscription, sculpt, trim, wear — and six Roman objects that carry it: the Vexillum (cloth from a crossbar), the Signum (pole standard with phalerae), the Flammula (the rank streamer), the Velarium (awning cloth over the stands), the Scutum and the gladiator's Parma. Mottos go on a tabula ansata. No heater shields, no heraldic divisions: dyed wool, painted fresco, gold leaf, square capitals.

§3

One design, many uses

a house picks ONE design; each place carries its own Roman object

Who owns which design: Manager house = m.flag (rivals derive theirs as today, from id + city) · online Domus = D.look89 grown to the full design minus sculpt (his 4a); its rank streamer stays the system design, and from Ludus Magnus up the house parma rides on its crossbar (his 1a) · Studio = customFlags, chosen by a character (banner91), a lanista or a career. Families keep their derived design (kin share it).
